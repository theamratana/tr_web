SaaS Platform

ROLUUN

ROLUUN is the all-in-one mobile store management app built for Cambodian entrepreneurs — helping store owners run their business smoothly from a single phone. From product catalog and stock management to walk-in POS sales, delivery orders, and employee access control, ROLUUN covers the full retail workflow. Stores go live in under 2 minutes with no technical knowledge required, and the platform supports 25 provinces across Cambodia. Free to start with a Pro plan at $9.99/mo for unlimited everything.

  • Store Profile: brand identity, logo, category & contact setup
  • Product & Stock Management: catalog, prices, images & low-stock alerts (Pro)
  • Walk-in POS & Delivery Orders: real-time status tracking & partner assignment
  • Employee Management: team roles, permissions & access control
  • Delivery Partner Setup: custom fees by district/province across Cambodia
  • Sales Dashboard: daily, weekly & monthly revenue reports
  • Digital Menu & eCommerce Listing: public marketplace & shareable menu link (Pro)
  • Available on Google Play & App Store — 100% free to start
